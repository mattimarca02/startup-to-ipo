## 3. Icon brief

- **File:** square, at least 512 x 512 (upload larger, e.g. 1024 x 1024, it gets scaled down). No text, no logo lettering, no UI.
- **One clear subject:** a single creature head, centred, filling about 70% of the frame. Recommended: the **Unicorn CEO** wearing a tiny tie, looking confident. Alternative: Coding Cat holding a glowing egg.
- **Palette (high contrast):** a deep navy or purple background (`#1B1464` to `#2D1B7E`), a bright warm yellow or gold glow behind the subject (`#FFC93C`), the creature in saturated white, pink and cyan, thin dark outline. No more than 4 main colours.
- **Mood:** a happy, cheeky face with eyes looking toward the viewer. Faces get clicks.
- **Small-size test:** shrink it to 64 x 64 px and then 48 x 48 px. You must still see a creature, a bright glow and a dark background. If the tie, eyes or outline vanish, simplify. Check it on a light and a dark phone screen too.
- **Don't:** use small details, text, several characters, gradients that turn muddy, or anything that looks like another game's icon.
- **Existing art:** `icons/` has the pass icons (VIP, Double Cash, Headhunter and so on). Keep the store icon in the same art style as those.

## 4. Thumbnail shot list (4 thumbnails)

Roblox thumbnails are 16:9, minimum 1920 x 1080 recommended. Take them in Studio using a **Play** session (F5), then **Test → Device** off, and hide the Studio panels (View → close Explorer, Properties and Output). Use a clean new account's save, so the HUD isn't cluttered. Take the screenshot with the in-game screenshot tool or a screen capture at 1920 x 1080.

Keep the same three things in all of them: the creature in the foreground, one clear action, a bright background. Add captions afterwards in an image editor, never inside the game.

### Thumbnail 1: The hatch

- **Scene:** the player's garage, with a Startup Egg cracking open on the incubator, light spilling out.
- **Place:** a Coding Cat or **Unicorn CEO** popping out of the egg with the hatch reveal mid-animation. Add 2 or 3 employees already walking around behind it.
- **Camera:** low angle, close to the incubator, slightly looking up, so the creature looks big. Egg centred on the left third, empty space on the right for a caption.
- **UI to show:** the **EGGS** button highlighted in the HUD and the egg odds panel open, showing the percentages. Nothing else open.
- **Caption idea:** "Hatch your team"

### Thumbnail 2: The poaching moment

- **Scene:** two players' offices side by side, in the same server (use two Studio clients in Test → Clients and Servers, or a friend).
- **Place:** Player A's **Venture Dragon** or **Server Sloth** walking toward Player B's door. Show the poaching offer window with the creature, price and the chance to win.
- **Camera:** medium shot from slightly behind and above Player B's shoulder, so the viewer sees both the creature and the offer window. Add a bright yellow "!" or the Headhunter icon on the offer.
- **UI to show:** the **POACH** panel open with a negotiation in progress. Pop-up message at the bottom centre such as "Offer accepted" or a counter-offer prompt.
- **Caption idea:** "Steal their star"

### Thumbnail 3: Your office is full

- **Scene:** a fully upgraded office, packed with at least 8 different creatures at work (capybara, cat, pigeon, shark, parrot, sloth, llama, dragon).
- **Place:** a wide shot with all the Rare, Epic and Legendary creatures near the front, the Unicorn CEO at the main desk.
- **Camera:** wide, high isometric-style angle, about 45 degrees down, so you see the whole office and the creatures.
- **UI to show:** the Cash counter showing a big number and the income per second. Close every window.
- **Caption idea:** "Collect all 12" (or "Build your dream team")

### Thumbnail 4: The IPO

> **Blocked until the IPO is built (ROADMAP M5).** Don't capture this one before that.

- **Scene:** the IPO celebration: the player's company on the stock exchange moment.
- **Place:** the whole team lined up in front of the office with the Unicorn CEO in the centre, confetti or gold coins in the air, and the IPO bell or ticker sign above them.
- **Camera:** low, wide, heroic angle looking up at the team, with the sign or bell in the top third.
- **UI to show:** the IPO window or result with the new permanent multiplier visible (for example "x2 income").
- **Caption idea:** "Ring the bell" or "Go public"

## 6. Launch checklist (non-code)

> **All of these are MY manual tasks (the owner of the Roblox account).** Claude cannot do them. Start the first four now, because they take time (see ROADMAP "Launch requirements").

### Account and eligibility

- [ ] **ID verification** on the account that owns the game: Roblox Settings → Account Info → Verify My ID. Needed to unlock features such as selling items and getting paid.
- [ ] **2-step verification** switched on: Settings → Security. Also needed for monetising, and protects the account.
- [ ] **Roblox Plus for 2 or more months**, **or** pay the refundable fee, as the current Roblox requirement states. Check the exact wording and price in Creator Hub before paying. Start the 2 months early, the clock can't be rushed.

### Game settings in Creator Hub

- [ ] **Age-rating questionnaire** filled in honestly for the game (Creator Hub → the experience → Settings or Age Guidelines). Mention real-money items, random chances (eggs) and player-to-player interaction (poaching). Redo it after any big update.
- [ ] **Roblox group created BEFORE any revenue**, and the game moved to the group (the group fee is 100 Robux, see ROADMAP). Moving after earning is harder. Add the second collaborator with the correct role.
- [ ] **Check the game is public only when ready**, and the **Odds disclosure** of eggs and boosts is visible in-game (it is a design rule).

### Store page

- [ ] Upload the **icon** (section 3) and **4 thumbnails** (section 4).
- [ ] Paste the **title** and **description** (sections 1 and 2). Remove the "Add this section" note and the IPO text if the IPO isn't live.
- [ ] **Thumbnail A/B testing in Creator Hub:** in the experience's Media or Thumbnails page, add variant A and variant B, run the test for the time Creator Hub recommends, then keep the winner. Test one change at a time (the order, then the first image, then the icon).
- [ ] Add the **video** (section 5) to the store page as a trailer if the site allows it.

### Reminders

- Remember that new games first reach age-checked players aged 16+, so the teen and adult tone of the page matters (ROADMAP "Launch requirements").
- Never put the Roblox API key, `.env` or `.env.txt` in a screenshot or a clip.
