# Content Bible: names, jokes and a 5-hour script (draft for the owner to approve)

Branch `content-bible`. **Creative draft only**: no game code, no number, no sim change. Every price, Income and hour stays as in `docs/LONG_GAME_PROPOSAL.md` section 3 and `LONG_ITEMS` in `tools/economy_sim.py`. Within each kind, items are in **buying order**; item counts per rung were checked against `LONG_ITEMS` (offices, upgrades, desks, new hires all match). Approve or rename in the table at the end.

**Rules used**
- ✱ = name already shipped or saved (12 creatures, 6 Garage upgrades, 3 eggs): only its joke can change, unless you accept a save migration (decision 1).
- Tone: friendly, funny, readable at age 10. No real brands or people, no swearing. Fictional places and jobs only.
- **Surprise events** use the design numbers: solved = +6 s of Income, unsolved = -15% Income for 30 s. The clue always points to the same ✔ answer: **nothing is random, nothing is paid for** (D9-safe). The engine starts Surprise Events at Series A, so Garage and Seed ones are one-time story moments (decision 4).
- **Customer calls** are one tap or choice each (7 s of Income). Eight caller lines per rung; the game picks them in any order.
- A hire row is the *planned* creature; the egg roll stays random (or the fixed order for restricted players).

## 1. Garage

**Theme:** Two friends, one borrowed garage, zero money. The car has lived on the driveway for months. **Fantasy:** Be the scrappiest startup on the street: every upgrade is held together with tape.

**Office: The Garage.** A one-car garage with a rolling door that sticks halfway, string lights, and an oil stain shaped like a banana.

**Upgrades:** **Desk** ✱ — A door lying on two paint buckets. · **Coffee Machine** ✱ — Makes coffee. Mostly makes noise. · **Laptop** ✱ — Held together by stickers and hope. · **Server Rack** ✱ — Blinks nonstop. Nobody dares ask why. · **Whiteboard** ✱ — Arrows pointing nowhere. Perfect. · **Neon Logo** ✱ — Flickers, buzzes, looks great from far away.

**Desks:** **Paint-Bucket Desk** — Wobbles on Mondays. · **Lawn-Chair Desk** — Comes with a free sunburn.

**Hires:** **Coding Cat** ✱ — Types 90 words a minute. 80 are "meow". · **Bug Beetle** ✱ — Finds bugs. Is also a bug. Relatable.

**Eggs:** **Garage Egg** ✱ — Smells like fresh paint and big ideas.

**Decor:** **Beanbag Throne** — Sinks one inch an hour. · **Garage Gnome** — Guards the stapler. Takes it seriously. · **Pizza-Box Tower** — Stacked high. Fresh as of Tuesday.

**Titles:** **Garage Dreamer** — "I have a plan. It has arrows."


Also hatch from the Garage Egg (random rolls, not on the planned roster): **Coffee Capybara** ✱ "Interns at everything, sleeps at nothing." · **Pixel Pigeon** ✱ "Draws logos. Leaves small gifts on the roof." · **Hustle Hamster** ✱ "Runs the sales wheel all night." · **Memo Mole** ✱ "Knows where every memo is. Under the couch."

**Customer calls**

- **Dog-walker Dan** wants an app that tells him which dog is pulling.

- **Mr. Quill, retired pirate** wants a treasure-map app, "no clouds, please".

## What each rung adds for the four action types

**Answer** = tap the chip, **Choose** = pick an option, **Timing** = press at the right moment, **Targets** = tap what appears. Later tiers of a task come one and two rungs after its first appearance.

| Rung | Answer | Choose | Timing | Hit targets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garage | Customer Call (tap the chip, 7 s of Income) | — | — | — |
| Seed | Calls queue up to 3 | Interview (pick 1 of 3) | Ship a Feature (press in the green zone) | Bug Storm (tap the bugs) |
| Series A | Calls get a chain bonus | Investor Pitch, Surprise Event, Support Ticket, Demo & Contract | Feature timing gets faster | Bug Storm: bigger swarms |
| Series B | Calls: shorter time to answer | Product Feedback, 1-to-1 / Team Pizza, Press Interview | Viral Post (press on the beat) | Bug Storm tier 2 |
| Series C+D | Calls: VIP callers | Ad Campaign, Cash Crunch, Board Request, decorate the office | Product Launch (3 timed steps) | Package Pickup (tap the boxes) |
| Pre-IPO | All calls at full speed | Roadshow meeting 1 and 3 (choose the answer) | Roadshow meeting 2 (press on the pause) | Roadshow: tap the camera flashes |
