Design only (2026-10-05, branch `design-customize`): owner decision **D-F**, split out of docs/CUSTOMIZE_PROPOSAL.md (`CUST` below). No script, config or test changed. Line numbers: `main` `9798388`. **(estimate)** = not measured; **(placeholder)** = sized by the sims. The saved fields (`pokedex.active.told`, `rounds`; `card.rounds`, `stake`, `shares`; `pokedex.dust`), the `pokedex` loader, versioning and the run check stay in CUST 1.2; the module names (`CompanyServer`, `CompanyConfig`, `CompanyClient`, `CompanyRemotes.CompanyAction`) in CUST's "New names".

**Fixed by the owner:** **D-F** reaching a stage by valuation lets the player raise a round of a size they pick; it gives Cash only, costs dilution (fewer IPO Shares), and the room follows the building owned, not the stage (CUST 6). **Stake rule (decided 2026-10-05, option B):** the founder stake counts toward the stamp tiers, even after the Shares shop is complete (section 3). **Round Cash path (decided 2026-10-05):** paid like the daily gift: spendable Cash, valuation unchanged, never through `addEarnings` (DailyServer.lua:177-180; test daily_test.py:110). **Decided by the owner (2026-10-05):** the Pre-IPO row is 56 / 113 / 171 bp; Gold has no stake condition; Platinum keeps a stake minimum, 87.5% (placeholder), so Large-every-round still loses Platinum (section 3).

## 1. The rules

Every number is a **placeholder** for S8 (section 6); nothing is random (D9).
- **What opens a round:** the **valuation** (never Cash) reaching a stage after the first: Seed, Series A, Series B today (IpoConfig.lua:17-23), plus Series C+D and Pre-IPO with LONG_GAME's 7 stages. IPO-ready has no round: it is the IPO. The stage is `IpoConfig.stageIndex(ipo.valuation, ipo.count)` (IpoConfig.lua:151-159); it never falls during a run (IpoServer.lua:122-135).
- **A round gives Cash and nothing else:** no building move, no Income change. The stage's building is buyable in SHOP > Office once the stage is reached, raised or not; the room follows the building owned (CUST 6). The Cash buys anything.
- **Flow:** (1) stage reached: one message per stage per company (`told`, section 5). (2) The **Funding window**: 3 choices (Small, Medium, Large), each with its Cash, the share given away, the IPO Shares after it and the stake left; a choice that would take the total past the cap is locked. (3) **Raise** (pick, then confirm "Raise $X for Y%", Y = bp / 100 with two decimals, so 3 bp shows 0.03%): Cash at once. **Later** keeps the round open. **Skip this round** (with a confirm) closes it for this company.
- **One round per stage per company,** settled in stage order; an open round stays open at the next stage; all close at IPO-ready (raised Cash could then only feed the seed). **Never raising** is a full path: no dilution, all the Shares, "Bootstrapped: 100% stake" on the card, no extra reward.
- **Amount:** choice i gives `floor(IpoConfig.stageValuation(roundStage, ipo.count) x cashBp[i] / 10000)` (IpoConfig.lua:140-143; `cashBp` whole, 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000): the **round's own stage**, never the one the player is at when raising, and the IPO count (fixed during a run). Its dilution is that stage's row too. Never Cash, Income, passes or stamps. *Test:* a Seed round left open and raised at Series B pays the Seed amount and costs the Seed bp. **Cash packs** call `addEarnings` today (ShopServer.lua:540), so they open a round sooner, never a bigger one; after LONG_GAME PR 2 they are gifts (LG:93-94) and open nothing, and rounds go live only after it (section 7). **Total dilution capped at 4,000 bp** (`DilutionCap`): a raise that would take the total past it is refused. Income is untouched. Data: `CompanyConfig.Funding` (shared engine, keyed by `IpoConfig.Stages` keys), `Enabled`, `StudioOnly`.
- **Saved** (`pokedex.active`): `told[stageKey]`; `rounds[stageKey]` = `s`, `c` (choice), `cash`, `bp` (dilution in basis points, whole, 0-10000, **as applied**, never recomputed from a later config), `at`, `studio`. `D = min(DilutionCap, Σ bp)` in whole bp, derived on load. A round under an **unknown stage key** is kept and its valid `bp` still counts (a newer version's round dilutes an older version's IPO). A malformed record of a known stage counts as settled with `bp` 0 and a warning, never dropped (that would allow a second raise).
- **The card keeps** `rounds` as they were, `stake` (10000 - D in bp) and `shares` (paid after dilution and pass): "Founder stake 91% · 3 rounds · $X raised" or "Bootstrapped".

## 3. Shares and dilution

**Why a fixed price fails.** The first draft's fixed 3 / 7 / 12% at every stage ignores that the Cash (`cashPct x stageValuation`) grows about 4,750 times from Seed ($197K) to Pre-IPO ($937M) on LONG_GAME's ladder (LG:42-47). Against the stage's **gap** (the valuation still to earn before the next stage), a Large round is worth 0.94% at Seed, 6.29% at Series A, 9.74% at Series B, 5.85% at C+D and 30.46% at Pre-IPO, always for 12% (today's: table below). By gap share nobody should raise at Seed, everybody at Pre-IPO (but see the measured savings below).

**The curve: the price follows the time a round is worth** (every number a **placeholder** for S8):
- `g` = the round's Cash / the stage's gap = `cashPct x stageValuation(s) / (stageValuation(s+1) - stageValuation(s))`. Both ladders scale every threshold by one factor per IPO (IpoConfig.lua:140-143; LG:88), so `g` doesn't depend on the IPO count.
- `h` = the stage's share of the loop's engaged hours: data, `Funding.HoursShare[stageKey]`, from LONG_GAME's rung hours (LG:42-47, loop 59.8 h) or today's first run (Seed, Series A, Series B last 2, 1, 5 of 12 minutes; ECONOMY.md 3b) until S8 measures them.
- `u = g x h`: the round's Cash in loop time at that stage's earning rate. An upper bound on the time saved: the Cash never counts toward the valuation, it only buys Income sooner. **Measured (S8, robust), the savings are far smaller:** Large rounds save about 5.0% of a loop, against the formula's 12.18%; the Pre-IPO Large round about 0.6%, against 7.639%.
- `bp = max(1, floor(P x u x 10000))`, with `P` = 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 (Small / Medium / Large): % of Shares given per % of loop time.
- The bp are **whole numbers in a table** in `CompanyConfig.Funding` (written from S8, never computed with floats at run time: today's Seed Large is exactly 500 bp, but floats give 499). A test recomputes the table with exact fractions.

**No choice is dominated, at any stage.** The three choices give Cash 1 : 2 : 3 and cost 1.5 : 4.0 : 7.5 units, so each step up costs 1.5, then 2.5, then 3.5 per unit of time: strictly rising. **Shares in whole basis points.** `D` as in section 1 (whole bp). `base` = today's `IpoConfig.sharesFor(valuation, count, pass)` (IpoConfig.lua:168-172, whole Shares, pass included, unchanged). Paid = `floor(base x (10000 - D) / 10000)`, never `1 - D` as a float: `floor(100 x (1 - 0.34)) = 65` (`1 - 0.34` is 0.65999...), `floor(100 x 6600 / 10000) = 66`. Dilution comes after the pass: the same proportional loss, at most 1 Share of rounding apart from "before the pass"; `D = 0` returns `base` exactly, so old calls and tests are unchanged.

**The rounding case.** At IPO 1 today `base = 10`: `floor(10 x 9,700 / 10,000) = 9`, so a "3%" round costs 10%; a 3 bp LONG Seed round would cost 1 of 20 Shares. **Fix: round to the nearest Share and carry the rest.** `exact = base x (10000 - D) + dust` (dust in 1/10000 Share); paid = `floor((exact + 5000) / 10000)`; new `dust = exact - paid x 10000`, from -5000 to 4999, saved as `pokedex.dust` (player level, kept across IPOs; a bad value becomes 0 with a warning; written in the IPO transform's one save). IPO 1, base 10, D = 300: exact 97,000, paid 10, dust -3,000 (the next IPO pays 0.3 less). Over a player's history the Shares paid stay within half a Share of exact (no round free, none overcharged); the window shows what this IPO pays. *Alternative:* plain floor showing the real loss ("-1 Share, 10%"): simpler; small rounds overcharge.
